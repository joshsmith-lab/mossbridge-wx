INLAND PENDER-COASTAL PENDER-INLAND NEW HANOVER-
COASTAL NEW HANOVER-INLAND BRUNSWICK-
421 PM EST TUE FEB 24 2015

...ICE STORM WARNING IN EFFECT UNTIL 8 PM EST THIS EVENING...

THE NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE IN WILMINGTON HAS ISSUED AN ICE
STORM WARNING...WHICH IS IN EFFECT UNTIL 8 PM EST THIS EVENING.
THE WINTER WEATHER ADVISORY IS NO LONGER IN EFFECT.

* LOCATIONS...PENDER...NEW HANOVER...AND INLAND BRUNSWICK COUNTIES.

* HAZARD TYPES...FREEZING RAIN AND OCCASIONAL SLEET.

* ICE ACCUMULATIONS...AN ADDITIONAL .05 TO .10 INCHES...WITH
  STORM-TOTAL ICE ACCUMULATIONS AROUND A QUARTER OF AN INCH.

* TIMING...TAPERING OFF BY 8 PM.

* IMPACTS...ICE ACCUMULATION ON BRIDGES AND OVERPASSES. TREES AND
  POWER LINES WILL RECEIVE MODERATE ACCUMULATIONS OF ICE.

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

AN ICE STORM WARNING MEANS SEVERE WINTER WEATHER CONDITIONS ARE
EXPECTED OR OCCURRING. SIGNIFICANT AMOUNTS OF ICE ACCUMULATIONS
WILL MAKE TRAVEL EXTREMELY DANGEROUS OR IMPOSSIBLE. TRAVEL IS
STRONGLY DISCOURAGED.
